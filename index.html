        Account settings

        For credit cards and loans: charges and the balance owed show as positive numbers, and you type them that way. Only the display changes; stored amounts and the CSV export keep charges negative.

        Recurring transactions

        A recurring transaction is entered only when you press Enter here; nothing is added on its own. Entering adds a row for each date that has come due, at the end of the account's sheet.

        Enter the ending balance from the statement. Then tick the rows that appear on it until the difference is zero. The ticks are saved together when you press Finish.

        Transfer

        Adds one row to each account. The two stay linked: changing the date or amount of one changes the other, and deleting one deletes both.

        Export

        Downloads every sheet as CSV, plus import.json to import it back exactly. The file is your data, decrypted: keep it safe and delete it when you no longer need it.

        Change password

        Everything is encrypted again under the new password, which takes a few seconds, and you are logged out everywhere. If the new password is lost, the data cannot be recovered.

        Sessions

        Ends every login, in every browser, this one included.
